My interests are at the intersection of robotics and language. The direction I want to take next is open-vocabulary 3D semantic mapping with adaptive granularity — whether intermediate semantic representations kept during mapping let a robot answer natural-language queries about a scene without reprocessing what it already saw. More broadly I'm interested in large language models across reasoning, evaluation and efficient inference, and increasingly in AI safety.

I'm also an AI consultant at Nubax, where I serve open-weight models on in-house H100/H200 clusters and build RAG and multi-agent systems.
